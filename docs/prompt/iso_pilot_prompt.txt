=== 1. 整屋概念图（定风格用，不入库） iso_kitchen_hero / 3:4 ===
Strict 2:1 isometric (dimetric) projection, classic game isometric tile view, camera locked at 45
degrees rotation and about 30 degrees elevation. Parallel projection with NO vanishing point and NO
real perspective: distant objects stay exactly the same size as near ones, all parallel edges stay
parallel. The floor reads as a 2:1 diamond grid, twice as wide as tall.

Subject: a small cosy home kitchen shown as an open isometric diorama room. Only the two back walls
exist (left-back wall and right-back wall); the front two walls are removed so we see inside. Square
room about 5 by 5 floor tiles. Cream plaster walls (#FFF1DC), pale warm tiled floor (#F2E3C9) with a
subtle diamond tile grid. Against the left-back wall: a chunky rounded cartoon refrigerator in cream
and warm wood, plus a small wicker vegetable basket hanging on the wall. Against the right-back wall:
a wooden cooking counter with a stove and a pot. On the floor: a white foam crate full of whole fresh
vegetables - whole cabbages, whole carrots, one whole eggplant, all complete and uncut, never sliced.
Standing in the middle of the room: a cute chibi girl about 2.5 heads tall, big friendly eyes, long
dark-brown hair, wearing a warm orange apron over a cream shirt, smiling, holding a small basket.
Beside her a tiny adorable cabbage mascot with little legs and a happy face. An open doorway in the
right-back wall.

Character turnaround sheet for an isometric game, matching the exact art style of the reference
image: same clean medium-thick warm-brown outlines, same flat soft cel shading, same bright cream
palette, same chunky simplified cartoon shapes, same tiny 2.5-head chibi proportions as the little
